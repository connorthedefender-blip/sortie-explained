Privacy Policy

Sortie Explained Uploader · last updated November 5, 2026

Sortie Explained Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by Connor Skluzacek. It is used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channel, Sortie Explained. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the owner's videos to his channel and set their title, description, privacy and publish time;
  • read the status of those videos to confirm they were processed and scheduled;
  • read YouTube Analytics for the owner's channel (for example views, watch time and audience retention).

How the information is used and stored

The information is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show him how they perform in a private dashboard that only he can open. Access credentials are kept in protected environment variables, never in public files. The app does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone, and it does not use the data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account permissions page.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops the app from reaching the channel. Stored analytics can be deleted on request to the contact below.
