Privacy Policy

Sortie Explained Uploader and Studio Uploader 2 · last updated November 8, 2026

Sortie Explained Uploader and Studio Uploader 2 ("the apps") are private tools operated by Connor Skluzacek. They are used only by their owner to manage his own YouTube channels: Sortie Explained, Front & Fault, Product Insight and Proof by Pictures. They are not offered to other users.

What the apps access

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the apps use YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the owner's videos to his channels and set their title, description, privacy and publish time;
  • read the status of those videos to confirm they were processed and scheduled;
  • read YouTube Analytics for the owner's channels (for example views, watch time and audience retention).

How the information is used and stored

The information is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show him how they perform in a private dashboard that only he can open. Access credentials are kept in protected environment variables, never in public files. The apps do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone, and they do not use the data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The apps use YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account permissions page.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops the apps from reaching the channels. Stored analytics can be deleted on request to the contact below.
